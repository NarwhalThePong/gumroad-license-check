## How the math works

```
lodging, each night   = actual hotel cost before taxes, up to the GSA maximum for that month
M&IE, full days       = GSA M&IE rate for the work location, for the fiscal year that day falls in
M&IE, first/last day  = 75% of the rate
meals provided        = minus the GSA breakdown amount for each meal, never below the incidentals amount
total per diem        = lodging + M&IE
```

Example with the default inputs: New York City, leaving Tuesday November 3, 2026 and home Friday November 6, 2026. In FY2027 the NYC lodging maximum for November is $358 and M&IE is $92, so lodging is 3 x $358 = $1,074, M&IE is $69 + $92 + $92 + $69 = $322, and the **total per diem is $1,396**. If lunch is provided at a conference on November 4, that day's M&IE drops by $26 to $66.

## The rules this follows

- **Lodging is a maximum, not an allowance.** You get your actual hotel cost up to the GSA rate for that night, and the unused part can't be moved to meals. Lodging taxes aren't included in CONUS rates; federal travelers claim them separately (GSA per diem FAQ, FTR 301-11.16).
- **75% on the first and last day.** For trips of 24 hours or more, the day you leave and the day you return get 75% of the M&IE rate; full days get 100%. A one-day trip longer than 12 hours gets 75% (FTR 301-11.20).
- **Meals provided are deducted.** When the government provides a meal or it's included in a registration fee, you deduct the GSA breakdown amount for that meal. On the first and last day the full meal amount comes off the 75% rate, but you always keep the incidentals amount. Meals from an airline or a free hotel breakfast don't count (FTR 301-11.21).
- **The work location sets the rate.** The rate is based on where the work activities are, not where you sleep, unless the agency authorizes the rate where lodging is obtained. If a city isn't listed, check its county; if neither is listed, the standard rate applies.
- **Fiscal years.** GSA rates run October 1 to September 30, and each day uses its own fiscal year's table, so a trip across October 1 can change rates mid-trip.
- **Continental US only.** Alaska, Hawaii and US territories use Department of Defense rates, and foreign travel uses State Department rates, which aren't included here.
- **Companies and contractors** often reimburse at GSA rates, but the exact policy is theirs. If you're self-employed and deducting meals, use the [self-employed per diem meal deduction calculator](self-employed-per-diem-meal-deduction-calculator), which follows IRS rules instead.
