Fees get all the attention, but for a new seller the bigger surprise is often timing. Some platforms hold your money for a week or two and pay out on a fixed day. Others never hold it at all, because the sale goes straight into your own PayPal or Stripe account. If you're counting on the first $100 to pay a bill, that difference matters.

We read each company's own help pages on October 20, 2026. Schedules change, so check the linked pages before you rely on a date.

## The short version

- **Fastest:** [Payhip](https://help.payhip.com/article/396-resolve-payment-issues-as-a-seller) and [Sellfy](https://docs.sellfy.com/article/42-how-to-receive-payments-from-customers) don't hold your money. A PayPal sale lands in your PayPal balance right away, and a card sale goes to your Stripe account and then to your bank on Stripe's schedule.
- **Gumroad:** sales sit in your Gumroad balance, then get paid weekly (or monthly, quarterly, or daily for some US sellers) once you have at least $100. A US bank payout goes out on Thursday and covers sales through the previous Friday.
- **Etsy:** new sellers usually wait 14 days before a sale can be deposited; established shops can get funds the next business day. Deposits go out daily, weekly, every two weeks or monthly.
- **Lemon Squeezy:** twice a month. Sales are held 13 days and paid on the 14th and 28th, with a $50 minimum.

When the money sits in your own PayPal or Stripe account (Payhip, Sellfy), you still have one more step: getting it to your bank. In the US, a standard [PayPal transfer to your bank](https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/paypal-consumer-fees) is free and usually takes 1 to 3 business days, while an Instant Transfer costs 1.75% (minimum $0.25, maximum $25). [Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts) usually pays a US account's first payout 7 to 14 days after the first live payment, and after that settles card payments in 2 business days and pays out daily by default.

## One sale, traced to the bank

Say you sell a $30 template on **Tuesday, October 6, 2026**, you're in the US, and your balance is already over each platform's minimum. Here's when the money is sent to you:

| Platform and payout method | Money sent to you | Days after the sale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payhip or Sellfy, buyer pays with PayPal | Oct 6, into your PayPal balance | 0 (plus 1–3 business days to move it to your bank for free) |
| Payhip or Sellfy, buyer pays by card through Stripe (account past its first payout) | about Oct 8, Stripe pays out to your bank | about 2 business days |
| Gumroad, weekly, US bank | Thursday, Oct 15 (covers sales through Friday, Oct 9) | 9, then a few business days at the bank |
| Gumroad, weekly, PayPal (only offered where Gumroad can't pay to a bank, so not for US sellers) | Friday, Oct 16, minus the 2% PayPal payout fee | 10, then usually 1–3 business days |
| Etsy, established shop, weekly | Monday, Oct 12 | 6, then up to 5 business days at the bank |
| Etsy, new seller, weekly | Monday, Oct 26 (funds eligible Oct 20) | 20, then up to 5 business days |
| Lemon Squeezy, US bank | Wednesday, Oct 28 (payout created Oct 15) | 22, then 1–5 days at the bank |

A sale one weekday later can shift the dates a lot. On Gumroad, a sale on Saturday, October 10 misses the Friday cutoff and goes out a week later, on Thursday, October 22. On Lemon Squeezy, a sale on October 16 waits for the payout created on November 1.

## What slows a first payout down

- **Gumroad:** a new account goes through a review that "can take 1–3 weeks", and Stripe identity checks (photo ID, proof of address, and an SSN for US sellers) must be complete. Payouts wait until your balance reaches $100.
- **Payhip and Sellfy:** your very first PayPal payment may be held while PayPal approves it, and your first Stripe payments stay pending for a few days while Stripe reviews the account.
- **Etsy:** adding or changing your bank account starts a 5-day hold, and Etsy may place a reserve on a new shop. Etsy says reserves apply to physical items, so a digital-only shop is less exposed.
- **Lemon Squeezy:** a payout under $50 stays pending and rolls into the next cycle.

## Which one to choose if timing matters

1. You need money within days of the first sale: Payhip or Sellfy with PayPal connected, since the platform never holds it.
2. You're already on Gumroad: get past the $100 minimum and the account review first. Gumroad pays to a bank account wherever it can; PayPal payouts, with their 2% fee, are only offered in countries without bank payouts. If you connect your own PayPal at checkout, Gumroad says PayPal-paid sales are credited to that account at the time of sale.
3. You sell on Etsy: plan on about two weeks of waiting until your shop is established.
4. You use Lemon Squeezy: plan around two paydays a month and up to about 3 weeks for a single sale.

Whatever you use, track sales by the day they happened, not the day the deposit arrives. A deposit is net of fees and often covers many sales, so it can't tell you what you sold or what you'll owe in tax.
