Practical, code-first guides for people who sell software, code and communities on Gumroad. Each one covers a gotcha in Gumroad's API that most setups miss.

## Free tools

- [Gumroad fee calculator](gumroad-fee-calculator): see what you keep on direct and Discover sales, and the price you need for a target payout
- [Gumroad bundle & discount calculator](gumroad-bundle-discount-calculator): price a bundle or offer code and compare your payout with selling each product separately

## Guides

- [Block refunded and charged-back buyers in your Gumroad license check](refunded-gumroad-license-keys) (Node and Python)
- [Sell access to a private GitHub repo on Gumroad](sell-private-github-repo-on-gumroad): invite on sale, remove on refund
- [Get Gumroad sale alerts in Discord, Slack or Telegram](gumroad-sale-alerts-discord-slack-telegram)
- [Sell a paid Discord on Gumroad and revoke roles on refund](gumroad-discord-roles-refunds)
- [Gumroad fees in 2026, explained](gumroad-fees-explained): what you keep on a $5, $19 and $49 sale, and how to price around the fee
- [Gumroad 1099-K and taxes](gumroad-1099-k-taxes): reconcile gross sales, fees and refunds for Schedule C
- [Gumroad vs Lemon Squeezy vs Polar vs Paddle](gumroad-vs-lemon-squeezy-polar-paddle-fees): fees in 2026 and what you keep on a $9, $29 and $99 sale
- [Sync Gumroad sales to Google Sheets automatically](sync-gumroad-sales-to-google-sheets): a free Apps Script that keeps refunds up to date
- [Gumroad Ping (webhook) reference](gumroad-ping-webhook): every field, refund and cancellation events, retries, and verifying unsigned pings (Node and Python)
