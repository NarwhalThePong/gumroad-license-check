If you already have a blog, a portfolio or a WordPress site, you may not want to send buyers off to a marketplace page. You want a buy button on your own page, a checkout that works, and a file that reaches the buyer automatically. SendOwl, Payhip, Sellfy and systeme.io can all do that, but in different ways: some put the checkout in a pop-up on your page, some send the buyer to a hosted checkout, and one of them has no embeddable checkout at all.

We read each platform's pricing page and help articles on October 20, 2026, with Gumroad as a reference point. We left out limited-time promotions. Prices and features change, so check the linked pages before you decide.

## The short version

- **The most complete checkout to plug into your site:** [SendOwl](https://www.sendowl.com/pricing) gives you Buy now, Add to cart and View cart buttons, payment links and an overlay checkout that keeps buyers on your page, plus PDF stamping and license keys, on every plan. It's also the most expensive to start ($39 a month).
- **Free to start, tax handled:** [Payhip](https://payhip.com/pricing) has a $0 plan (5% per sale) with an embeddable buy button and direct checkout links, and it collects and remits EU/UK VAT and US/Canadian sales tax for you.
- **Flat fee, store widgets you can drop in:** [Sellfy](https://sellfy.com/pricing/) lets you embed a single button, a product card or your whole store, with checkout in a pop-up on your site and no transaction fee, from $29 a month ($22 billed yearly).
- **Lowest cost per sale, but no embedded checkout:** [systeme.io](https://systeme.io/pricing) charges 0% even on its free plan, but you link from your site to a systeme.io order page instead of embedding a checkout, and you deliver the file from the thank-you page or by email.
- **Reference:** [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/help/article/44-build-gumroad-into-your-website) has overlay and embed widgets too, but the checkout itself always happens on Gumroad.

## What it costs: the same worked example

Assumptions, matching our other guides: $20 products, US domestic cards through Stripe at 2.9% + 30¢, monthly billing unless noted, sales tax left out. That's $22.00 of processing a month at $500 (25 sales) and $132.00 at $3,000 (150 sales). International cards and PayPal cost more on every platform.

What this shows:
- **systeme.io is cheapest in both examples** because it adds nothing on top of processing. You give up the embedded checkout and SendOwl-style file protection for that.
- **At $500 a month Payhip Free is the cheapest option with a real embeddable button on monthly billing,** because it has no monthly fee. If you pay for a year up front, Sellfy Starter ($44.00) edges it out by $3. Payhip Plus beats Payhip Free above about $967 a month.
- **At $3,000 a month Sellfy, SendOwl and Payhip land within $10 of each other** on monthly billing, so pick on features rather than price. If your sales go even slightly over $36,000 a year, SendOwl moves you to Scale ($159), which adds $72 a month.

## Who each one is best for

**[SendOwl](https://www.sendowl.com/pricing)** is best if your website is the shop: you want a cart across several products, an overlay checkout on your own page, and protection for what you sell (PDF stamping, license keys for software, drip content). Every feature is on every plan, so you only upgrade for higher sales and order limits. SendOwl also lists Shopify, WordPress and Notion integrations.

**[Payhip](https://payhip.com/pricing)** is best if you want to start for $0 and don't want to think about VAT and sales tax: Payhip collects and remits them for you. Use its embed button or direct checkout links for single products on your site, and link to your Payhip store when buyers want to browse or buy several things.

**[Sellfy](https://sellfy.com/pricing/)** is best if you'd rather paste a ready-made product card or your whole store into a page than build product pages yourself, and you'd like a flat monthly fee with no transaction fee. It also sells subscriptions and print-on-demand merch alongside downloads.

**[systeme.io](https://systeme.io/pricing)** is best if you're happy to send buyers from your site to a separate sales page and checkout, and want the lowest cost per sale plus email and funnels in the same tool. See our [systeme.io vs Kit vs Payhip](systeme-io-vs-kit-vs-payhip-free-plans) guide for its free-plan limits.

**[Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/help/article/44-build-gumroad-into-your-website)** is the simplest to set up if you're fine with checkout happening on Gumroad, and it's the only one here that acts as merchant of record. It costs the most per sale in both examples.

## The honest downsides

**SendOwl**
- The highest starting price: $39 a month, or $32.50 billed annually. The pricing page shows a free trial but no free plan.
- Two caps per plan (sales and orders, over a trailing 12 months); you're upgraded automatically when you pass one.
- Download bandwidth is limited per month (10 GB, 20 GB and 50 GB on the plan cards) and overages are charged.
- Subscriptions can't go in the cart.

**Payhip**
- 5% per sale on the free plan; processing is extra on every plan.
- The embed button doesn't cover courses, memberships, coaching or bundles, and multi-product carts from your own site are unclear (see the table).
- The button needs a script in your page head, which some site builders make awkward.

**Sellfy**
- No free plan, only a 14-day free trial.
- Yearly sales caps ($10k, $50k, $200k); Sellfy says it may charge a 2% overage fee if you pass one and don't upgrade.
- Starter keeps Sellfy branding and leaves out upsells, cart abandonment and an affiliate program.

**systeme.io**
- No embeddable checkout: buyers leave your page for the systeme.io order page.
- File delivery is a thank-you-page link or an email you set up yourself; email attachments are capped at 5 MB, and the free plan allows only 1 automation rule.
- The free plan stops at 2,000 contacts.

**Gumroad** (where our own products are sold)
- Checkout always happens on Gumroad, even with the overlay.
- About 16.9% of sales in both examples once card processing is added.

## How to choose in two minutes

1. You want buyers to stay on your page and fill a cart: SendOwl, or Sellfy if you want product cards and a flat fee.
2. You sell a few downloads and want a $0 start with tax handled: Payhip Free, then Plus above about $967 a month.
3. You're fine linking out to a sales page and want the lowest fees: systeme.io Free.
4. You sell software or worry about PDFs being shared: SendOwl (license keys and PDF stamping on every plan).
5. Before you commit, open the platform's pricing page. The numbers above were correct on October 20, 2026.
