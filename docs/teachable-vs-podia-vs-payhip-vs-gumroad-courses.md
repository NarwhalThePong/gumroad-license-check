A course platform charges you in up to three ways: a monthly plan, a percentage of each sale, and card processing. Which one is cheapest depends almost entirely on how much you sell. A free plan with a 5% fee wins when you're small; a flat plan with no fee wins once sales grow.

We compared the published pricing of Teachable, Podia, Payhip and Gumroad on October 20, 2026. We left out limited-time promotions because they expire. Prices change, so check the linked pricing page before you decide.

## The short version

- **Just starting, selling under about $1,000 a month:** [Payhip's free plan](https://payhip.com/pricing) costs the least in our examples: $0 a month plus 5% of each sale and card processing. Every plan has the same features, including drip lessons, quizzes and certificates.
- **Selling about $2,000 a month or more and want a dedicated course platform:** [Teachable Builder](https://www.teachable.com/pricing) is $69 a month billed yearly ($89 monthly) with no Teachable transaction fee when you use Teachable Payments. It was the cheapest option in our $3,000-a-month example.
- **Courses plus a community, email list and website in one place:** [Podia Shaker](https://www.podia.com/pricing) is $84 a month billed yearly ($99 monthly) with no Podia transaction fee and email for up to 500 subscribers.
- **No budget, testing an idea, or selling a simple video or PDF course:** [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/pricing) has no monthly fee and handles sales tax for you, but its 10% + $0.50 fee, plus 2.9% + $0.30 card processing on direct sales, makes it the most expensive here once you sell regularly.

## What a month really costs: a worked example

Assumptions: you sell a **$100 course** to US buyers paying by card. Card processing is 2.9% + $0.30, so $3.20 per sale on every platform. Payhip includes its $5-a-month video hosting (embed your videos from YouTube or Vimeo instead and take $5 off). Teachable and Podia include video. Each plan is at its cheapest billing option unless noted.

Two things stand out. At $500 a month, a free plan with a percentage fee beats every paid plan, and Teachable's and Podia's entry plans cost almost twice as much as Payhip Free. At $3,000 a month the order flips: the flat plans with no platform fee (Teachable Builder, Podia Shaker) come out cheapest, and Gumroad costs about $246 a month more than Teachable Builder, close to $3,000 over a year.

**Where the plans cross over** (same $100 course, ignoring card processing, which is the same everywhere, and Payhip's optional $5 video hosting):
- Payhip Plus beats Payhip Free above about $967 a month (3% of sales covers the $29).
- Teachable Builder (yearly) beats Payhip Free above $1,380 a month, and beats Payhip Plus above $2,000 a month.
- Payhip Pro beats Payhip Plus above $3,500 a month.
- Gumroad's per-sale $0.50 hurts more on cheap courses: at $20 a course, Gumroad's fee is 12.5% of each sale before processing.

## Who each one is best for

**[Teachable](https://www.teachable.com/pricing)** is best for course creators who already sell steadily (roughly $2,000 a month or more) and want a dedicated course school: iOS and Android student apps, drip content, quizzes, upsells and cart recovery, and tax handling built in. Builder adds a built-in affiliate program for your own students and partners.

**[Podia](https://www.podia.com/pricing)** is best for creators who want courses, a community, an email list, a website and digital downloads under one login. Every plan includes community spaces, coaching, events, a website and blog with a custom domain, and email up to the plan's subscriber limit.

**[Payhip](https://payhip.com/pricing)** is best for new course sellers and anyone selling courses next to ebooks, templates or memberships. All plans have the same features: a branded course player, seven lesson types (video, text, quiz, download, survey, assignment, embed), drip lessons, free preview lessons, preorders, payment plans and certificates. You only upgrade to lower the transaction fee.

**[Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/pricing)** is best for testing a course idea with no monthly bill, for simple video-and-PDF courses, and for sellers who want Gumroad to act as merchant of record and deal with sales tax worldwide. Its Discover marketplace can bring new buyers, at a 30% fee on those sales.

## The honest downsides

**Teachable**
- Starter's 7.5% fee is the highest percentage here, and Starter allows only 5 products.
- The 0% fee on Builder and up applies when you use Teachable Payments.
- Course certificates and removing Teachable branding are listed on Growth ($139 a month billed yearly) in the plan summaries.
- PayPal sales cost more to process (3.49% + 49¢ in the US).

**Podia**
- Mover charges 5% and includes email for only 100 subscribers.
- Affiliate marketing, upsells and PayPal checkout start on Shaker.
- Refunds: Podia refunds only if you ask within 72 hours of your most recent payment, and no partial refunds on yearly plans.

**Payhip** (where we also sell some of our products)
- Hosting videos on Payhip costs $5 a month for 16 hours; longer libraries need more, or you embed from YouTube or Vimeo.
- We didn't find a mobile student app on its pricing or course pages.
- Pro ($99 a month) only pays off above $3,500 a month in sales.

**Gumroad** (where our own products are sold)
- 10% + $0.50 per direct sale, plus card processing, is the most expensive option once you sell regularly; Discover marketplace sales cost 30%.
- Its course content is organized as pages of text, files and videos. We didn't find quizzes or certificates in its help center, and timed lessons go out by scheduled email (Workflows) rather than a lesson-by-lesson drip.

## How to choose in two minutes

1. Estimate your monthly course sales honestly. If you haven't sold yet, plan for the small case.
2. Under about $1,000 a month: Payhip Free (or Gumroad if you want marketplace exposure or worldwide sales-tax handling as merchant of record).
3. Between about $1,000 and $2,000 a month: Payhip Plus.
4. Above about $2,000 a month: Teachable Builder or Podia Shaker on yearly billing, depending on whether you want a dedicated course school (Teachable) or courses plus community and email in one place (Podia).
5. Before you commit, read the current pricing page. The numbers above were correct on October 20, 2026.
