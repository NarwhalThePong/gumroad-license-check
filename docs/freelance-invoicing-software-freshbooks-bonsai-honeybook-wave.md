Every invoicing tool has two prices: the plan, and the cut it takes when a client pays online. For most freelancers the second one is bigger, and it depends far more on *how* your client pays (card or bank transfer) than on which tool you pick.

We compared the published pricing of FreshBooks, Bonsai, HoneyBook and Wave on October 20, 2026. We left out limited-time promotions because they expire. Prices change, so check the linked pricing page before you decide.

## The short version

- **Cheapest, just need invoices and simple books:** [Wave's Starter plan](https://www.waveapps.com/pricing) is $0 a month with unlimited invoices and estimates. You pay only when a client pays online (2.9% + $0.60 per card payment, 1% per bank payment with a $1 minimum).
- **Invoicing plus real accounting, few clients:** [FreshBooks Lite](https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing) is $23 a month and covers invoices to 5 clients, expenses, estimates and tax-time reports. More clients means Plus ($43) or Premium ($70).
- **Proposals, contracts and invoices in one flow:** [Bonsai Essentials](https://www.hellobonsai.com/pricing) is $19 a month billed yearly ($25 monthly) per user. Its cheaper Basic plan doesn't include invoices.
- **Client-facing businesses that book, sign and get paid in one place (photographers, coaches, planners):** [HoneyBook Starter](https://www.honeybook.com/pricing) is $29 a month billed yearly, with unlimited clients, but its bank-transfer fee (1.5%) is the highest of the four.

## What a month really costs: a worked example

Assumptions: you send **3 invoices of $1,000** a month ($3,000 billed) to 3 clients, and you use each tool's cheapest plan that sends invoices, at its cheapest billing option. Card fees use the standard card rate (not Amex or business cards).

**If clients pay by bank transfer (ACH):**

| Tool and plan | Plan cost | Payment fees | Total a month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wave Starter | $0 | 3 × 1% × $1,000 = $30.00 | **$30.00** |
| Wave Pro, yearly | $15.83 ($190 ÷ 12) | $30.00 | $45.83 |
| Bonsai Essentials, yearly | $19.00 | $30.00 | $49.00 |
| FreshBooks Lite | $23.00 | $30.00 | $53.00 |
| HoneyBook Starter, yearly | $29.00 | 3 × 1.5% × $1,000 = $45.00 | $74.00 |

**If clients pay by card:**

| Tool and plan | Plan cost | Payment fees | Total a month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wave Starter | $0 | 3 × ($29.00 + $0.60) = $88.80 | **$88.80** |
| Wave Pro, yearly | $15.83 | 3 × $29.00 = $87.00 (first 10 payments; we assume yearly Pro gets the same monthly allowance) | $102.83 |
| Bonsai Essentials, yearly | $19.00 | 3 × ($29.00 + $0.30) = $87.90 | $106.90 |
| FreshBooks Lite | $23.00 | $87.90 | $110.90 |
| HoneyBook Starter, yearly | $29.00 | 3 × ($29.00 + $0.25) = $87.75 | $116.75 |

Two things stand out. First, asking clients to pay by bank transfer saves about $58 a month on $3,000 of invoices with any of these tools, far more than the difference between plans. Second, the per-payment cuts are nearly identical; what separates the tools is the plan price and what the plan includes.

## Who each one is best for

**[FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing)** is best for freelancers who want proper accounting (expenses, reports, tax-time summaries) alongside invoicing and have only a handful of regular clients. The 30-day trial needs no card, so you can send real invoices before paying.

**[Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com/pricing)** is best for freelancers and small studios whose work starts with a proposal and a contract. Essentials puts proposals, contracts, invoices, a client portal and expense tracking in one plan with unlimited clients.

**[HoneyBook](https://www.honeybook.com/pricing)** is best for service businesses where clients book a time, sign, and pay a deposit in one go. Every plan includes unlimited clients, proposals and contracts, a client portal and payment features such as autopay, payment plans and automatic late fees.

**[Wave](https://www.waveapps.com/pricing)** is best for anyone who wants to spend $0 a month. Starter covers unlimited invoices, estimates and bookkeeping records; Pro ($19 a month) adds automatic late-payment reminders, bank-transaction import and removes Wave branding.

## The honest downsides

**FreshBooks**
- Lite only lets you invoice 5 clients; 6 or more means Plus at $43 a month.
- Proposals and retainers start on Plus.
- AutoPay card payments are charged a higher "managed payments" rate (3.5% + $0.30).

**Bonsai**
- The $9 (yearly) Basic plan doesn't include invoices, so the real starting price for invoicing is $19 a month billed yearly, or $25 monthly.
- Prices are per user.
- Using Stripe or PayPal instead of Bonsai Payments adds a 1% platform fee, and getting paid in another currency adds 2.5%.

**HoneyBook**
- The highest starting price of the four, and bank transfers cost 1.5% instead of 1%.
- Removing "Powered by HoneyBook" and automations need Essentials ($49 a month billed yearly).

**Wave**
- Starter adds $0.60 to every card payment, the highest fixed fee here.
- Instant payouts weren't compared for Wave.
- Online payments need approval (identity check and credit review).
- Live chat and email support are weekday business hours (Eastern).

## How to choose in two minutes

1. Decide how your clients will pay. If you can get them to pay by bank transfer, every tool gets much cheaper.
2. Under $0 budget, or only a few invoices a month: Wave Starter.
3. Want accounting and tax reports with invoicing, 5 clients or fewer: FreshBooks Lite.
4. Work starts with a proposal and contract: Bonsai Essentials.
5. Clients book sessions and pay deposits: HoneyBook Starter.
6. Before you commit, read the current pricing page. The numbers above were correct on October 20, 2026.
