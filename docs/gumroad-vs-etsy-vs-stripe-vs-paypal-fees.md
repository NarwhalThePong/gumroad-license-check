If you sell a digital product (a template, ebook, preset, course or code) the platform's cut decides how much of each sale you keep. This page puts the **US standard fees as of Oct 20, 2026** for Gumroad, Etsy, Stripe, PayPal and Shopify side by side, then works through a $10, $25 and $50 sale.

## The fees at a glance (US, as of Oct 20, 2026)

| Platform | What it is | Fee per sale | Monthly fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gumroad, direct sale | Storefront + checkout, merchant of record | 10% + $0.50 | none |
| Gumroad, Discover sale | Gumroad's marketplace finds the buyer | 30% | none |
| Etsy | Marketplace | $0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + 3% + $0.25 processing | none required |
| Etsy, order from an Offsite Ad | Marketplace, buyer came from an Etsy ad elsewhere | the above + 15% (12% once the shop has made $10,000 in a 365-day period), max $100 per order | none required |
| Stripe, domestic card | Payment processor | 2.9% + $0.30 | none |
| PayPal Checkout | Payment processor | 3.49% + $0.49 | none |
| PayPal, standard card payments | Payment processor | 2.99% + $0.49 | none |
| Shopify Basic, Shopify Payments online card | Store builder + payments | 2.9% + $0.30 | $39/month, or $29/month billed yearly |

A few things the table doesn't show:

- **Gumroad** is merchant of record, so it handles sales tax and VAT on your sales. Discover's 30% applies only when a new customer finds you through Gumroad's marketplace; sales from your own links pay 10% + $0.50.
- **Etsy** charges the 6.5% and the processing fee on the whole order, and processing is charged on sales tax too. The listing fee is $0.20 per listing, and an auto-renewing listing is renewed for $0.20 each time it sells. Details: [Etsy fees explained](etsy-fees-explained).
- **Stripe and PayPal** only process the payment. You need your own site or checkout page, and sales tax is your responsibility. PayPal adds 1.50% for international buyers; Stripe lists its own international and currency conversion fees. Details: [PayPal & Stripe fees explained](paypal-stripe-fees-explained).
- **Shopify** charges a monthly plan on top of the card rate. On Basic, Shopify Payments online cards are 2.9% + 30¢ (premium cards 3.5% + 30¢, international cards +1%), and using a third-party payment provider instead of Shopify Payments adds a 2% transaction fee. Higher plans have lower card rates.

## Worked example: a $10, $25 and $50 digital product

Example math only: one item, a US buyer, no sales tax, no shipping, standard rates from the sources below. Each fee is rounded to the cent. Shopify's monthly plan fee isn't included in the per-sale numbers.

| Platform | $10 sale: fee, you keep | $25 sale: fee, you keep | $50 sale: fee, you keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gumroad, direct | $1.50, keep **$8.50** (15.0%) | $3.00, keep **$22.00** (12.0%) | $5.50, keep **$44.50** (11.0%) |
| Gumroad, Discover | $3.00, keep **$7.00** (30.0%) | $7.50, keep **$17.50** (30.0%) | $15.00, keep **$35.00** (30.0%) |
| Etsy | $1.40, keep **$8.60** (14.0%) | $2.83, keep **$22.17** (11.3%) | $5.20, keep **$44.80** (10.4%) |
| Etsy, Offsite Ads order (15%) | $2.90, keep **$7.10** (29.0%) | $6.58, keep **$18.42** (26.3%) | $12.70, keep **$37.30** (25.4%) |
| Stripe card | $0.59, keep **$9.41** (5.9%) | $1.03, keep **$23.97** (4.1%) | $1.75, keep **$48.25** (3.5%) |
| PayPal Checkout | $0.84, keep **$9.16** (8.4%) | $1.36, keep **$23.64** (5.4%) | $2.24, keep **$47.76** (4.5%) |
| Shopify Basic card (plus plan fee) | $0.59, keep **$9.41** (5.9%) | $1.03, keep **$23.97** (4.1%) | $1.75, keep **$48.25** (3.5%) |

Percentages are the effective fee rate (fee / price).

How the $25 numbers are built:

- **Gumroad direct:** $25 x 10% = $2.50, plus $0.50 = **$3.00**.
- **Etsy:** listing $0.20 + transaction $25 x 6.5% = $1.625, rounded to $1.63 + processing $25 x 3% + $0.25 = $1.00. Total **$2.83**. With an Offsite Ads order, add $25 x 15% = $3.75 for **$6.58**.
- **Stripe:** $25 x 2.9% = $0.725, plus $0.30 = $1.025, rounded to **$1.03**.
- **PayPal Checkout:** $25 x 3.49% = $0.8725, plus $0.49 = $1.3625, rounded to **$1.36**.
- **Shopify Basic:** same card rate as Stripe, so **$1.03** per sale, plus the plan. Spread over 20 sales a month, the $39 monthly plan adds $1.95 per sale ($1.45 on the $29 yearly-billed rate).

## What the numbers mean

- **Processors are cheapest per sale, but you build the rest.** Stripe and PayPal take the least, but you need a site, a way to deliver the file and a plan for sales tax.
- **Gumroad and Etsy cost more and do more.** Both host the product page and checkout and deliver the file. Gumroad also handles sales tax as merchant of record; Etsy brings marketplace buyers. Gumroad's flat $0.50 makes it relatively expensive on cheap products; at $50 it's close to Etsy without Offsite Ads.
- **Marketplace sales cost the most.** A Gumroad Discover sale (30%) or an Etsy Offsite Ads order (an extra 15% or 12%) takes a much bigger cut, in exchange for a buyer you didn't bring yourself.
- **Shopify's cost depends on volume.** The card rate matches Stripe's, but the monthly plan matters most when you sell a few low-priced items a month.

## FAQ

### Which is cheapest for selling digital products?

Per sale, at US standard rates, Stripe card payments (2.9% + $0.30) are the lowest of the options here, and Shopify Basic matches that card rate but adds a monthly plan. PayPal Checkout is a little higher. Gumroad and Etsy cost more per sale but include the storefront, checkout and file delivery.

### What exactly does Gumroad charge?

Gumroad's pricing page lists 10% + $0.50 per transaction for direct sales and 30% for Discover sales, with no monthly fee. Payout methods can have their own fees, so check a real payout once. See [Gumroad fees in 2026, explained](gumroad-fees-explained).

### Can I sell digital downloads on Etsy?

Yes, and the same listing, transaction and processing fees apply. There's no shipping, so the fees are charged on the item price (and processing on any sales tax). Try your own price in the [Etsy fee calculator](etsy-fee-calculator).

### Do these rates apply outside the US?

No. Every platform has different rates by country, and negotiated or volume pricing differs. Check the official pages below for your country.
