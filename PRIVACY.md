# Privacy Policy: Creator Payout Calculator
Effective 2026-10-19.
- The extension does not collect, sell, or share personal data, and it has no analytics or ads.
- Prices, tax rates and custom fee profiles are stored only on your device (chrome.storage.local).
- If you enter a Pro license key, it is sent only to Gumroad's license API (api.gumroad.com) to confirm the purchase, and again every few days. Gumroad's privacy policy covers that request.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
Contact: through the seller's Gumroad page, https://paudrudis.gumroad.com
